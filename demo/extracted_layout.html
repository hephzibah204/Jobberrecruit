Job description

Paystack is hiring a Product Designer in Lagos to craft intuitive experiences across our payment products. You will work closely with product and engineering to design flows used by businesses across Africa.

Responsibilities

  • Design user flows, wireframes, and high-fidelity interfaces.
  • Run usability testing and iterate on feedback.
  • Maintain and extend the design system.
  • Collaborate with product and engineering from concept to ship.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience.
  • 2–5 years of product design experience (mid level).
  • Strong portfolio across web and mobile.
  • Proficiency in Figma and modern design tooling.
