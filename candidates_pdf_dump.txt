the job.

Candidates should also be able to receive a weekly premium-jobs digest.
For example:

Premium jobs posted Monday–Friday are collected.

Candidates receive a weekly overview/digest of those premium jobs.



unnecessary emails for every premium job if the weekly-digest option is being used.

12. AI Strengthen Achievements /
Professional Summary / Generate Bullets

The system is generating random/generic suggestions instead of improving the
candidate's existing content.
For example, when a candidate enters:
"I recruit for Jobber Recruit"
and asks the AI to strengthen it, the current system generates unrelated
interpretations such as treating "This is how to improve that you recruit for Jobber
Recruit" as a generic term rather than understanding the candidate's actual
employment context and elaborating it.
Required AI behaviour


================ PAGE 9 ================
The AI must:
1.
Read the candidate's existing content.
2. Understand the context.
3. Improve the existing wording.
4. Preserve the underlying facts.
5. Produce a professional version directly relevant to the candidate.
6. Avoid inventing unrelated experience or organisations.
7. Avoid generic suggestions unrelated to the supplied text.
Strengthen Achievements
When the candidate clicks Strengthen, the AI should improve the achievement
already entered.
It should not provide several unrelated generic answers.
Generate Bullets
When Generate Bullets is selected:

Convert the candidate's existing information into relevant professional bullet
points.

Keep the content factually connected to the original information.

Do not invent responsibilities or achievements.
Professional Summary Improvement
The AI should improve the candidate's actual professional summary rather than
replacing it with a generic profile.

14. Tailor Resume to a Job
The Tailor to a Job functionality is not working properly and does not appear to be
correctly connected to the AI. See the pics above of our HTML we shared.
Required AI behaviour
The AI should tailor the candidate's resume based on the specific relevant job, not
every job posted on JobberRecruit.
The system should:
1.
Identify jobs relevant to the candidate/profile.
2. Allow the candidate to select the relevant job.
3. Analyse the selected job description.
4. Compare the job requirements against the candidate's resume.
5. Tailor the resume around the selected job.
6. Preserve truthful candidate information.
7. Highlight relevant experience, skills and achievements.
8. Avoid inserting irrelevant content from unrelated job postings.
The final result should clearly be a targeted version of the candidate's CV for the
selected vacancy.
