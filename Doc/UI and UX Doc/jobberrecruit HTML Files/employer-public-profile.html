About Paystack

Paystack is a financial technology company building modern payment infrastructure for businesses in Africa. Founded in 2015 and acquired by Stripe in 2020, Paystack helps businesses in Nigeria, Ghana, South Africa, and Kenya process payments securely and reliably.

From small startups to large enterprises, over 200,000 businesses use Paystack to accept payments online and offline, manage subscriptions, and expand across borders. Our team is passionate about making commerce accessible to every African business.

Our Hiring Process

Stage 1: CV review — we review every application within 7 days.
Stage 2: 30-minute video call with a member of the People team.
Stage 3: Technical or skills assessment (role-specific, ~2 hours).
Stage 4: Final panel interview with the hiring manager and team lead.
Offer: We aim to give a decision within 10 business days of your first interview.
