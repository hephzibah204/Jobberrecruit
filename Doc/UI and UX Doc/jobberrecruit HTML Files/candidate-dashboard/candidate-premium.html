Common questions

What happens when I subscribe?

You get instant access to the AI Resume Builder, AI Career Tools, and unlimited practice tests. Your subscription renews monthly at ₦5,000 until you cancel.

Do aptitude test certificates come with Premium?

No. Certificates are only issued for paid courses you complete and pass. Practice aptitude tests help you learn and self-assess, but don't carry a certificate — this keeps our certificates credible to employers.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from this page whenever you like — you'll keep Premium access until the end of your current billing month.

Are employer-invited tests free?

Always. When an employer invites you to take an aptitude test as part of hiring, it's completely free — that never counts against your practice-test limit.
