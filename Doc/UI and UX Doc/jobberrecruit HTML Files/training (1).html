Got questions?

Frequently asked questions

Are all courses free?

No. JobberRecruit offers both free and paid courses. Free courses are accessible immediately after enrolling; paid courses unlock once payment is confirmed via Paystack or Flutterwave.

Do I get a certificate after completing a course?

Certificates are awarded on paid courses only, issued upon completion within roughly 3 days. Free courses let you learn the material but do not include a certificate — upgrade to a paid course if you need one for your CV.

What's the difference between a Course and an eBook?

A Course is delivered as video lessons (via YouTube or an uploaded file) with a stated duration. An eBook is a downloadable PDF guide you read at your own pace. Either type carries a certificate if it's a paid item — certificates are not issued for free items.

Can I access courses on my phone?

Yes. All courses, including YouTube-hosted lessons and uploaded files, are fully accessible on mobile browsers — no separate app required.
