Role overview

We're seeking a Sales & Procurement Manager to support day-to-day sales activities and procurement processes, working closely with the Founder. The role includes managing customer orders, coordinating suppliers, and tracking inventory.

Qualifications

  • Minimum of 2 years experience in sales, operations, procurement, or a related role
  • Comfortable working with numbers and spreadsheets
  • Willingness to learn and take ownership of tasks
  • Strong communication and organizational skills
  • Interest in retail, homeware, or product-based businesses
