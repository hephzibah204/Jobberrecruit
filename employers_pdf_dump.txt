2. My Jobs – Repost Job Function
Employers should be able to repost an existing/previously posted job from the My
Jobs section.
Reposting rules
Reposting should not be free.
For example:

If an employer posted a job one week ago and wants to repost it, the employer
must pay for the repost.

For Pay-as-you-go employers, the reposting fee should be deducted/charged
according to the normal job-posting fee.

For employers on a relevant subscription plan, the repost should consume the
applicable posting allowance/credit from their subscription.

The system should create a new posting cycle rather than simply changing the
original posting date without recording the repost transaction.
Job editing restriction
Employers should only be allowed to edit a newly posted job within one hour of
posting.
After the one-hour window:

Editing should be disabled.

The employer should be instructed to contact JobberRecruit Support if
changes are required.

Support should be able to assist with the required changes.

4.2 Candidate Search Ranking


================ PAGE 4 ================
The candidate search results should prioritise candidates who are most useful and
most recently active.
Recommended ranking priority
Candidates should generally appear in this order:
1.
Candidates with a mostly/completely completed profile
2. Candidates who have uploaded a CV
3. Candidates with the most recent login/activity
4. Candidates with relevant skills/qualifications matching the employer's search
5. Other eligible candidates
The objective is to ensure that employers see the strongest and most active
candidates first rather than an arbitrary candidate order.
4.3 Candidate Visibility / Activity Filter
Add candidate visibility/activity filters such as:

Visible this week

Visible last week

Visible this month

Visible in the last 3 months

Older/inactive profiles
The search results should use the candidate's latest login/activity/profile visibility
information to determine the appropriate category.
4.4 Candidate Eligibility for Employer Search
We recommend that only candidates who have substantially/completely completed
their profile should appear in the employer candidate search.
At minimum, the system should verify the required profile information before making
a candidate searchable.
Candidates with incomplete profiles should not be prioritised over qualified,
completed profiles.

4.7 Unlimited Access Plan
The Unlimited Access Plan is different from the standard subscription plan.
Employers subscribed to the Unlimited Access Plan should have:
